I am an (untenured) Associate Professor of Finance at the School of Economics and Management, **Wuhan University**. My research lies at the intersection of **macroeconomics** and **asset pricing**, with current work on the real cost channel of monetary transmission, fiscal policy under the zero lower bound, and the empirics of energy and cryptocurrency markets. I also serve as Associate Editor at *[Financial Economics Letters](https://www.anserpress.org/journal/fel)*.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the **National University of Singapore** in 2023, advised by [Jordan Roulleau-Pasdeloup](https://sites.google.com/site/jordanroulleaupasdeloup/). Before that I read finance at Jinan University for my master's degree, working with [Yonghong Jiang](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Yonghong-Jiang).

My ongoing projects are funded by the **National Natural Science Foundation of China** (2025–2027) and the **Ministry of Education Humanities and Social Science Fund** (2025–2027), both as PI. Recent work has appeared in *Review of Economic Dynamics*, *Macroeconomic Dynamics*, *Energy Economics*, and *Economics Letters*.

I advise undergraduate honors theses in finance at Wuhan University. A current list of advisees and their placements is available [here]({{ '/assets/pdf/Supervision_He.pdf' | relative_url }}).
